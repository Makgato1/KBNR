OUR STORY

Who We Are

KBNR is a dynamic tax and accounting firm founded by two young, professional black women. Integrity, professionalism, commitment and transparency are the core principles that drive KBNR's efforts to grow within the tax and accounting industry while simplifying our clients' day-to-day business.

The founders' vision is to provide an exceptional service that enables clients to uphold high standards of financial reporting and tax compliance.

Professional service. Meaningful relationships. Financial confidence.

Meet Our Team

FOUNDING PROFESSIONAL

Nomawothi Bafana

Known as Noma

Nomawothi Bafana, known as Noma, is a highly qualified tax practitioner with a remarkable educational background, including a Bachelor's degree in Financial Sciences, an Honours degree in Taxation, and a Master's degree in Taxation from the University of Pretoria.

As a member of the South African Institute of Taxation (SAIT) and a registered general tax practitioner, Noma brings over five years of experience in tax planning, compliance and strategy.

Her commitment to continuous professional education and staying updated on ever-changing tax laws allows her to deliver exceptional services, assisting clients in minimising tax liabilities while ensuring full compliance.

Beyond her professional achievements, her passion for promoting a healthy lifestyle as a qualified fitness trainer reflects her balanced and dedicated approach to life, making her an invaluable asset to KBNR and its clients.

FOUNDING PROFESSIONAL

Kusaselihle Maphosa

Known as Sasa

Kusaselihle Maphosa CA(SA), also known as Sasa, is a qualified chartered accountant CA(SA) with over 5 years of experience, known for her exceptional organizational skills and self-driven attitude.

She possesses a deep passion sfor the evolving field of accounting and has excelled in the diverse auditing space, working with companies in the Industrial, Pharmaceutical, and Automotive industries.

Throughout her career, she has gained extensive insights within the Finance domain, showcasing expertise in financial statement preparation, accounting reporting, risk assessment, business strategy, going concern evaluation, application of IFRS requirements, and Taxation.

Proactive, innovative, and highly influential, Sasa has a remarkable track record of consistently enhancing businesses' financial reporting, internal controls, and overall financial position, making her an invaluable asset to KBNR and its clients.
